# Pronunciation data

The site's main pronunciation source is the General American list from
open-dict-data/ipa-dict (MIT):
https://github.com/open-dict-data/ipa-dict
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-dict-data/ipa-dict/master/data/en_US.txt

The original license is included in IPA-DICT-LICENSE.txt.

Source notation is normalized for learner readability: r and l are used for
phonemic /ɹ/ and /ɫ/; stressed CMU AH uses /ʌ/, while unstressed r-coloured
schwa uses /ɚ/. This is a broad phonemic guide, not an audio transcription.

Gaps and contextual readings were reviewed, with Cambridge Dictionary US
pronunciations used as a reference where available:
https://dictionary.cambridge.org/

Phrase entries combine the dictionary forms of constituent words, without
attempting to transcribe connected speech, sentence stress or weak forms.
The entries minute, resume, subject to and live up to include contextual
readings where the default dictionary order could otherwise mislead.
